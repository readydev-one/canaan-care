About Canaan Care

Compassionate care, delivered with integrity

We believe every person deserves to feel safe, respected, and supported in the comfort of their own home. At Canaan Care, that belief guides everything we do.

Our story

Where we come from

Canaan Care was founded on a simple but profound conviction: that quality home care should feel like family — not a service. We saw a gap between the clinical and the compassionate, and built our organization to bridge it.

From our earliest days, we have been guided by the needs of the families we serve. Every policy, every training, every hire has been shaped by the real experiences of real people navigating some of life's most vulnerable moments.

"We don't just care for people in their homes. We care about them as people."

Today, Canaan Care is trusted by families across our community to provide reliable, dignified, and heartfelt support — and that trust is something we never take for granted.

Our values

What we stand for

These aren't just words on a wall. They shape every decision we make and every interaction we have with the people in our care.

Dignity

Every client is treated as a whole person — with respect, warmth, and full attention.

Integrity

We do what we say. Families trust us with their most precious people — we honor that.

Community

We are rooted in the communities we serve, and committed to giving back to them.

Excellence

We hold our standards high — because the people in our care deserve nothing less.
